# Privacy Policy for Havana Göre Giyin
**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026
	Welcome to **Havana Göre Giyin**! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Location Data:** We collect coarse and precise location data to provide accurate real-time weather forecasts and display outfit recommendations appropriate for your current area.
- **Photos and User Content:** When you share your daily outfit on the World Style Map or My Style Gallery, the uploaded photos and optional outfit notes are stored in our secure database.
- **User Credentials & ID:** Anonymous user IDs and social handles are stored to manage your outfit posts and profile preferences.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To display accurate weather data and personalized outfit suggestions.
- To enable style sharing on the World Style Map and My Style Gallery.
- To authenticate user accounts and manage user-generated content.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We use trusted third-party service providers solely to operate app features:
- **Open-Meteo API:** For weather forecasting.
- **Firebase (Google Cloud):** For secure database storage and user authentication.
- **Apple / Google Payments:** For processing optional in-app purchases.

---

## 4. User Safety & Content Moderation
We enforce strict User-Generated Content (UGC) safety policies:
- Users can report inappropriate content or block abusive users at any time.
- Objectionable content and abusive behavior are strictly prohibited.
